# Missing Link: scroll sequence plan

One idea per screen. Each section plays its beats in order and never shows everything at once.
Reference studied: learnwithzero.com (no header on load, scene first, shape-mask transition into the
next act, one stat per screen with a curved logo wall, tabbed selector, timeline that draws as you scroll).

## Act 0 · Load (no header, scroll locked for about 2.5s)
1. Empty dark stage.
2. Chains slide in from both sides and settle with a gap.
3. Headline wipes in line by line: "Your marketing has / a missing link."
4. Scroll cue fades in. Scroll unlocks.

## Act 1 · The chain (pinned hero)
| Beat | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline leaves. Chains close in, the empty slot starts to glow | Drag to turn the chain, hover lifts a link |
| 2 | "Most marketing breaks in the gap…" then the "What you plan" / "What you get" labels | |
| 3 | Text clears. The magenta link flies in on its own | |
| 4 | Lock: flash, the chain pulls taut, sparks burst | Click the magenta link to pulse it |
| 5 | "We find it — and fix it." then the subheadline, then the CTAs | |
| 6 | The link opens into a portal that fills the screen | |
| 7 | The header slides in only now | |

## Act 2 · Proof (pinned)
One number at a time: 23+ years → 8 industries → [X] engagements. Below, a curved 3D wall of client
names turns as you scroll. Interactions: drag the wall, click the dots to jump between numbers.

## Act 3 · "Which of these sounds like you?"
Heading, then three quotes one by one, then the matching service panel.
Interactions: tabbed selector (auto-advances with a progress bar until you click), each panel has its own
animated visual, and "Start with …" prefills the form.

## Act 4 · Belief statement (pinned)
Words light up one at a time while the section holds still. A soft light follows the cursor.

## Act 5 · Senior thinking. Real delivery. (pinned, three beats)
Intelligence → Experience → Execution. Each beat lights one link of a three-link chain; by the end
all three are joined. Interactions: click 01 / 02 / 03 or a link to jump.

## Act 6 · Selected work (pinned, horizontal)
Cards slide past one at a time with a 01 / 04 counter. Interactions: cards tilt toward the cursor, and the
links in each card's artwork close on hover.

## Act 7 · Four steps (timeline)
A line draws down the page. Each step lights its node and swaps the sticky visual on the right
(radar → priorities → launch → growth chart). Interaction: click a step to jump to it.

## Act 8 · Team
Cards arrive one by one, tilt toward the cursor and reveal their focus areas on hover.

## Act 9 · Questions
One answer open at a time, with an animated open and close.

## Act 10 · Diagnostic (finale)
The Bridge mark builds as you fill the form: name draws the left link, email draws the right link,
organisation shows the gap, and a valid request snaps the magenta link in. This closes the story
the hero started.
